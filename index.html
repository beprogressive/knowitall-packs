KnowItAll

KnowItAll is a Chrome extension for learning vocabulary while reading. It highlights the words you do not know yet on the pages you read, translates a word in its context when you ask, and lets you mark words as known until the page is clear.

How it uses Google Drive

Connecting Google Drive is optional. If you connect it, the extension keeps one file, “KnowItAll dictionary.json”, in your Drive, so that your known words, language profiles and settings are the same on all your devices and survive a reinstall. It asks only for access to files it created itself and cannot see anything else in your Drive.

Privacy

There is no KnowItAll server, no account and no tracking. See the privacy policy.
