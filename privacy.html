KnowItAll privacy policy

Last updated: 24 November 2026

KnowItAll is a Chrome extension. It has no server of its own. Its developer receives none of your data.

What the extension stores

Your known words, the times you marked them, your language profiles and your settings. They are kept in your browser. The extension does not store the pages you visit, their addresses or your browsing history.

Page text and translation

The text of the pages you read is processed on your device to find unknown words. Translations use the language models built into Chrome.

Chrome sync

If Chrome sync is on, Chrome copies the stored data through your Google account to your other devices.

Google Drive (optional)

Only if you connect Google Drive in the options of the extension, and only with your consent, it uses the Google Drive API with the drive.file permission, which covers only files the extension created.

  • Data accessed: the file “KnowItAll dictionary.json” that the extension creates (and duplicates of it, should two devices create one at the same time), with its name, identifier, creation time and checksum. No other file in your Drive.
  • Use: only to keep your known words, language profiles and settings the same on your devices.
  • Sharing: none. The data travels directly between your browser and Google over HTTPS. It is not sent to the developer or to anyone else, not sold, not used for advertising and not used to train AI models.
  • Storage and protection: the file is in your own Drive, under your control. Access tokens are managed by Chrome; the extension does not store them.
  • Retention and deletion: the file stays until you delete it. Disconnecting in the options stops sync on that device and leaves the file. Delete the file in Google Drive at any time, and withdraw the permission in your Google account. Removing the extension deletes its data from that browser.

KnowItAll's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Language packs

Language packs and dictionaries are downloaded from GitHub, which, like any website, sees the address the request comes from. Nothing about you or your words is sent with these downloads.

Changes and contact

Changes to this policy are published on this page. Questions: GitHub issues.
